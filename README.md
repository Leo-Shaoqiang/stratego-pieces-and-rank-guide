# Stratego Pieces & Rank Guide

Interactive guide to all 12 classic Stratego pieces, rank comparison (Classic 1-10 vs Modern 10-1), piece counts, movement limits, and combat matchup interactions.

🔗 **Official Piece Guide**: [stratego pieces](https://strategocheatsheets.com/pieces)  
🔗 **Live Combat Calculator**: [Stratego Battle Judge](https://strategocheatsheets.com/)

---

## 🎖️ Piece Roster & Counts (40 Pieces per Player)
- **1x Marshall** (Rank 1 Classic / Rank 10 Modern)
- **1x General** (Rank 2 Classic / Rank 9 Modern)
- **2x Colonels** (Rank 3 Classic / Rank 8 Modern)
- **3x Majors** (Rank 4 Classic / Rank 7 Modern)
- **4x Captains** (Rank 5 Classic / Rank 6 Modern)
- **4x Lieutenants** (Rank 6 Classic / Rank 5 Modern)
- **4x Sergeants** (Rank 7 Classic / Rank 4 Modern)
- **5x Miners** (Rank 8 Classic / Rank 3 Modern) - Disarms Bombs
- **8x Scouts** (Rank 9 Classic / Rank 2 Modern) - Unlimited straight movement
- **1x Spy** (Rank S) - Assassinates Marshall when attacking
- **6x Bombs** (Stationary) - Eliminates any attacker except Miners
- **1x Flag** (Stationary) - Objective of the game
